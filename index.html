🏢 1. Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy governs the use of the UNIQON Billing Pro mobile and desktop application developed and published by UNIQON COMPUTERS KARAIKUDI ("we", "our", or "us").

UNIQON Billing Pro is designed as a secure, offline-first Point of Sale (POS), GST Invoicing, Inventory, and Job Sheet management software for retailers, service centers, and small to medium businesses.

100% Local Storage Principle All your core business data — including customer records, sales invoices, quotations, purchase records, inventory quantities, and accounting figures — is stored exclusively on your local device hardware using an encrypted local SQLite database. We do not collect, monitor, sell, or host your business transactions on any cloud server.

📱 2. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

To enable offline operational capabilities, UNIQON Billing Pro requests strictly minimal Android permissions as detailed below:

Permission Technical Name Purpose & Data Handling
Camera android.permission.CAMERA Used solely on-demand to:
• Scan 1D/2D product barcodes & QR codes quickly.
• Capture Job Sheet device intake photos and delivery confirmation.
Processing is 100% on-device. Images are never transmitted or streamed to remote servers.
Biometrics android.permission.USE_BIOMETRIC Used strictly to verify the shop owner or cashier's identity via Android's native BiometricPrompt (Fingerprint unlock).
The app does NOT access, read, or store raw biometric templates. Verification occurs entirely inside the device's secure hardware enclave (TEE/Keystore).
Local Network / Wi-Fi ACCESS_WIFI_STATE
INTERNET 		Used exclusively for Local Wi-Fi Peer Sync to synchronize invoices and inventory between desktop and mobile terminals on the same local area network (LAN/Wi-Fi).
No merchant transaction data is uploaded to internet cloud servers.

💾 3. Information You Provide & Store

When using the application, you may enter:

  • Business Profile: Business name, address, contact phone number, state, and GSTIN (used solely for printing receipts and invoices).
  • Customer & Supplier Info: Contact names, phone numbers, and billing addresses entered by you for generating bills and quotations.
  • Inventory & Transactions: Products, barcodes, prices, stock levels, sales bills, and job service records.
  • Security Credentials: A 6-digit App PIN and Recovery PIN stored securely using device-level encrypted storage (Flutter Secure Storage / Keystore).

🚫 4. Advertising & Third-Party Trackers

Zero Ads & Zero Behavioral Trackers:

  • UNIQON Billing Pro contains NO advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob, Unity, or Facebook Audience Network).
  • The application contains NO third-party analytics or user tracking SDKs.
  • We do NOT track your app usage, location, or customer behavior.
  • We do NOT sell, rent, trade, or monetize your business data.

📤 5. External Sharing & Integrations

When you choose to share an invoice, estimate, or receipt via WhatsApp or SMS, the app uses standard Android Intent mechanisms:

  • The action is triggered solely when you explicitly tap the WhatsApp or Share button.
  • The PDF or formatted text message is handed directly to the external WhatsApp or messaging app installed on your device. Once handed over, the data is governed by WhatsApp's privacy policy.

🗑️ 6. Data Retention, Backup & Deletion

You have 100% sovereignty over your data:

  • Local Backups: The app allows you to create password-protected or encrypted SQLite backups to a local folder or external drive chosen by you.
  • Data Deletion: You can delete specific customers, bills, or items at any time through the app.
  • Complete Removal: Uninstalling the application or selecting "Clear Data" in Android App Settings immediately and permanently deletes all local records from the device.

👶 7. Children's Privacy

UNIQON Billing Pro is a business software tool intended for merchants, retail business owners, and staff members. It is not directed at or designed for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children.
